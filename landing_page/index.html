How To Use Linko

Follow these 3 simple steps to start chatting and transferring files instantly.

1

Create Unique Identity

Launch Linko and enter your unique username (@username). No password required! Linko instantly sets your identity and signs you in.

2

Connect Over Wi-Fi or Relay

Linko automatically scans your local network for nearby peers using UDP discovery on port 53843. For long distance, connect seamlessly over E2EE relay.

3

Share Files, Chat & Vault

Send files at up to 60 MB/s, send view-once self-destructing text notes, play P2P Night Tic-Tac-Toe games, and hide private notes in your 4-digit PIN Secret Vault.
